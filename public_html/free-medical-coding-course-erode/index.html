Is the medical coding course in Erode really free?+
Yes. Under the Vetri 108 programme, 108 selected students in Erode receive a 100% scholarship (fully free training). Another 300 receive a 50% scholarship and 600 receive a 20% concession, so every eligible applicant gets some level of support.
Who can apply for the Erode medical coding scholarship?+
Freshers and graduates from B.Sc, B.Pharm, Nursing, BPT, Life Sciences, Bio-medical and most other streams can apply. No prior coding experience is required — the course is designed for beginners.
Do you provide placement support in Erode?+
Yes. Our placement team provides interview preparation, resume guidance and connections to medical coding and RCM employers recruiting in Erode and across Tamil Nadu.
Where is the Erode training conducted?+

How do I secure one of the 108 free seats in Erode?+
Submit the application form on this page. Every applicant is assessed for the highest scholarship tier they qualify for, and the 108 fully free seats are allotted to the top eligible candidates.
